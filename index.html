Why is wheel alignment important?

Incorrect Wheel Alignment or tracking, as it is often referred to, can result in rapid uneven tyre wear and can affect the handling and SAFETY of your vehicle as well as FUEL ECONOMY.

The vehicle manufacturers set the Wheel Alignment at the factory in order to provide a smooth comfortable ride for your vehicle and to get the maximum life from the tyres fitted. These settings can be affected by excessively worn steering and suspension parts, driving against a kerb, hitting a pothole or other objects on the road.

So it is worth keeping an eye out for unusual wear on your tyres, such as premature wear on the inside or outside shoulder of your tyres, which may be due to an incorrect Wheel Alignment setting. Or, pop on in for us to have a look.

Tip!

Wheel Alignment is a relatively inexpensive part of keeping your tyres in tip top condition and maintaining optimum handling for your vehicle, so have your vehicle's wheel alignment checked yearly by OUR specialists.

Tips
Why are tyre pressures important?

There are three primary reasons why maintaining correct tyre pressure is important. Number one is road safety; tyres that are under-inflated can overheat and lead to premature failure. Tyres that are over-inflated can lead to rapid uneven tread wear and poor vehicle handling on the road.

The second reason is cost. Incorrectly inflated tyres suffer more from the knocks and bumps a tyre is subjected to under daily use. Also, incorrectly inflated tyres take more power to maintain the same vehicle speed, which means you would save fuel on a journey when tyres are correctly inflated.

The third reason is longevity — checking and correcting your tyre pressures regularly will help you get the maximum life from your tyres, increasing the tyre's legal life.

Tip!

Check your tyre pressures regularly, every 2–4 weeks, including the spare. Check when tyres are cold, not after a long journey. We offer FREE tyre pressure checks to everyone, whether you bought your tyres from us or not.
