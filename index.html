Why alignment actually matters

A wheel that's out of alignment wears unevenly, pulls slightly on the road, and quietly costs you fuel — most people don't notice until the tyre's already half worn on one edge.

Factory alignment settings shift over time: kerbs, potholes, and worn suspension parts all knock it out gradually. If you're seeing uneven wear on the inside or outside of a tyre, that's usually the giveaway.

Tyre pressure, actually explained

Under-inflated tyres overheat and wear unevenly. Over-inflated ones make for a harsher ride and wear the centre of the tread faster than the edges. Either way, you're losing tyre life and fuel economy.

Check pressures cold — meaning before you've driven anywhere, not after a long trip when the tyres have heated up and the reading's skewed. Every 2–4 weeks is a good habit, spare included.
